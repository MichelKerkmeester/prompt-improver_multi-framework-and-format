# v1.5.0.0, Complexity Ratings Follow a Scored Rubric

The complexity rating now comes from a scored rubric in the DEPTH Complexity Assessment. A request whose stated load is heavy rates 9 or 10, and the header label scale gains Complex for that band.

## Why This Release

The skill rated a prompt's complexity from 1 to 10 under the constraint "Do not add unrequested complexity", and no rule said what earned a 9 or a 10. No deliverable in the Claude Sonnet 5 xhigh framework-coverage run of 2026-09-26 rated itself above 8/10, and the Project side rated lower than the skill. On the operator's decision, the Complexity Assessment now scores what the user stated.

## What's New at a Glance

- **The rating is 1 plus five scores.** The Complexity Assessment scores Outputs, Inputs, Rules, Audience and Stakes from 0 to 2, and only from what the user stated. The rating is 1 plus those five scores, capped at 10.
- **Stated facts are the only points that count.** Every point must point to something the user stated, so the constraint against unrequested complexity still holds. The framework the user names, the format command and a request for no questions do not count as rules.
- **Complex joins the label scale.** The header used to give Low, Medium or High, so a 9 or a 10 had no label of its own. The bands are now Low 1 to 4, Medium 5 to 6, High 7 to 8 and Complex 9 to 10, and the header gives the label, the number or both.
- **The skill, the guides and the kernel state the scale.** `SKILL.md`, the three format guides and the kernel's Delivery Protocol state the scale and point to the rubric. The four Project Knowledge mirrors of the three format guides state the complexity label too.
- **Structure tiers keep their ranges and drop the names.** The JSON and YAML guides named their structure tiers Simple 1 to 3, Medium 4 to 6 and Complex 7 to 10, which would call a 7 Complex. Those tiers now read as bare ranges with the same thresholds and structures.
- **A Complex rating still uses the 7 or more route.** The question flow is unchanged. Routing, commands, framework selection, the scoring gate and format lock do not change.
- **Recorded versions move forward.** `sk-prompt-improver/SKILL.md` moves from 1.4.0 to 1.5.0 and `claude project/Custom Instructions.md` moves from 1.5.0 to 1.6.0. `sk-prompt-improver/description.json` and `sk-prompt-improver/README.md` move from 1.4.0.0 to 1.5.0.0 and both Project README claims follow, as do `SYNC.md` and the system README's kernel, skill, changelog and release-notes lines, and the changelog now holds eight releases.
