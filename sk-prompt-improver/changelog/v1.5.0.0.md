## [**1.5.0.0**] - 2026-09-27

Complexity rubric release. The complexity rating now comes from a scored rubric in the DEPTH Complexity Assessment, so a request whose stated load is heavy rates 9 or 10, and the header label scale gains Complex for that band. `sk-prompt-improver/SKILL.md` moves 1.4.0 to 1.5.0, `claude project/Custom Instructions.md` moves 1.5.0 to 1.6.0.

> Scope: `references/depth-framework.md` (the Complexity Assessment and a new Complexity Rubric), the header rule in `SKILL.md`, the complexity label in the three format guides and their four Project Knowledge mirrors, the structure-tier names in the JSON and YAML guides, and the kernel's Delivery Protocol. No routing, command, framework-selection, scoring-gate, question-flow or format-lock behavior changes.

---

### A Rubric For The Complexity Rating

From the Claude Sonnet 5 xhigh framework-coverage run of 2026-09-26. The skill rated a prompt's complexity 1 to 10 under the constraint "Do not add unrequested complexity", and no rule said what earned a 9 or a 10. No deliverable in that run rated itself above 8/10, 27 of 48 missed the tier their scenario asked for, and the Project side rated lower than the skill. On the operator's decision, the Complexity Assessment now scores five dimensions 0 to 2 from what the user stated: Outputs, Inputs, Rules, Audience and Stakes. The rating is 1 plus the five scores, capped at 10. Every point must point to something the user stated, so the constraint against unrequested complexity still holds, and the framework the user names, the format command and a request for no questions do not count as rules.

&nbsp;

### Complex Joins The Label Scale

The header gave complexity as Low, Medium or High, so a 9 or 10 had no label of its own. The bands are now Low 1 to 4, Medium 5 to 6, High 7 to 8 and Complex 9 to 10, and the header gives the label, the number or both. `SKILL.md`, the three format guides and the kernel state the scale and point to the rubric. The JSON and YAML guides named their structure tiers Simple 1-3, Medium 4-6 and Complex 7-10, which would call a 7 Complex. Those tiers now read as bare ranges with the same thresholds and structures. The question flow is unchanged: a Complex rating still falls under its 7 or more route.
