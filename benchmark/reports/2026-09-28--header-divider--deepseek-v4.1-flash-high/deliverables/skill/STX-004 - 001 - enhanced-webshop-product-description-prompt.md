Mode: $refine | Complexity: Low (4/10) | Framework: COSTAR

---

**Context:** A webshop product page that shoppers scan on a phone before deciding. Copy that over-promises ("revolutionary", "must-have", "amazing!!") reduces trust and gets skipped, and copy that runs long gets skimmed and abandoned. The description must earn belief with concrete facts and stay short enough to read in one thumb-scroll.

**Objective:** Write one product description for {product_name} that informs and persuades without hype, so a busy shopper quickly understands what the product is, what it is made of, and why it is worth buying.

**Style:** Plain, factual, confident. Understated rather than enthusiastic. Let the product's own specifics carry the persuasion instead of adjectives.

**Tone:** Professional, calm, trustworthy. Never salesy.

**Audience:** {target_audience} — name their need and the moment they are shopping. If this is not supplied, infer the single most likely buyer from the product and name them explicitly; never write for "everyone".

**Inputs (fill from the product listing):**

- Product name: {product_name}
- Materials: {materials}
- Key facts: {key_features}
- Primary benefit: {main_benefit}
- Call to action: {cta}

**Requirements:**

- Length: {word_count} words. Default 60-90 words in a single short paragraph. Hard ceiling 120 words.
- Mobile-first: short sentences (roughly 15 words maximum), no walls of text, easy to scan.
- Structure: (1) a one-line statement of what the product is, (2) one factual sentence on the materials and craft, (3) one concrete benefit, (4) a single low-pressure call to action.
- Mention the materials {materials} naturally inside the description.

**Do:** Use concrete nouns and verifiable specifics. Sound like a knowledgeable salesperson who respects the buyer's time and intelligence.

**Don't:** Use exclamation marks, superlatives ("best", "amazing", "revolutionary"), hype or filler adjectives, urgency pressure ("buy now before it's gone"), or any claim the listing cannot support. Do not pad the copy with adjectives.

**Format:** Plain prose only. No headings, no bullet points, no preamble. Output the description and nothing else.
