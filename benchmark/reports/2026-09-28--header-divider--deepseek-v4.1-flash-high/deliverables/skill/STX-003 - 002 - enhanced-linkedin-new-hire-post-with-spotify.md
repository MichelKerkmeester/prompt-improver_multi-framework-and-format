Mode: $short | Complexity: Medium (5/10) | Framework: RCAF

---

**Role:** You are a warm, human-sounding social writer who writes LinkedIn posts that read like a real person talking, not a press release.

**Context:** We are announcing that Priya Nair has joined as our new Head of Design. Her first day is October 14, and she joins us from Spotify. The post is for our company's LinkedIn. The goal is a post that feels genuine and personal, so it invites real engagement rather than polished corporate applause.

**Action:** Write one LinkedIn post announcing Priya Nair's hire as Head of Design with her October 14 start date. Keep it warm and human. Specifically:
- Open with a genuine, specific note of welcome rather than a stock phrase.
- Name Priya and her role, state that she starts October 14, and mention that she joins us from Spotify.
- Say briefly and concretely why we are glad she is joining (e.g., what her craft or perspective brings), in plain language.
- Close with a short, sincere line that invites people to connect or say hello.

**Format:** One LinkedIn post, 80-150 words. Short paragraphs, plain everyday language, no more than one or two emoji at most (default to none). Optionally end with 2-3 relevant hashtags. Do not use corporate filler.

**Constraints:**
- Warm, not corporate: ban stock phrases such as "thrilled to announce," "excited to welcome," "we are delighted to share," and "dynamic leader."
- Mention her Spotify background naturally, without leaning on it as the whole point or over-selling it.
- Do not invent facts: if a detail is unknown (company name, specific projects), use a clearly marked placeholder like [company name] instead of making it up.
- No exclamation-heavy hype and no corporate jargon.

**Tone reference (aim for this):** "Priya Nair is joining us as our new Head of Design on October 14, coming over from Spotify. We've admired her work for a while, and we're glad she's bringing that eye for [craft/quality] to the team. If you know Priya, say hi. If you don't, you will soon."

**Out of scope:** Do not add a job description, a list of responsibilities, or announcement logistics such as where to apply or who to email.
