1. **App name + one-line description** — what it does and for whom.
2. **Audience** — existing users, a waitlist, cold prospects, press, or a mix?

4. **Tone** — e.g., excited/casual, polished/corporate, technical/credible.

Once I have these (even rough answers are fine), I'll produce a tight, ready-to-use prompt — delivered as a Canvas artifact — that you can run through an AI to get the actual email copy.
