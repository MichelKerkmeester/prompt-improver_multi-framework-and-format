Since markdown is your target format, here's the finalized prompt for a general-audience coffee brewing blog post:

```markdown
Write a ~1,200-word blog post in Markdown titled "Coffee Brewing for Beginners," 
aimed at a general audience of home coffee drinkers who currently use drip 
machines or instant coffee and want to try brewing manually for the first time.

## Structure

1. **Intro** (2–3 sentences) — why manual brewing is worth trying: better flavor, 
   more control, cheaper than café visits.

2. **The 4 core variables** — grind size, water temperature, coffee-to-water 
   ratio, and brew time. Explain each in plain language with beginner-friendly 
   starting numbers.

3. **Two beginner methods** (use H2/H3 headers):
   - Pour-over (e.g., V60-style)
   - French press
   
   For each: equipment needed, numbered step-by-step instructions, and one 
   common mistake to avoid.

4. **Starter equipment list** — budget-friendly gear (grinder, kettle, scale) 
   as a bulleted list with rough price ranges.

5. **Troubleshooting table or list** — quick fixes for bitter, sour, or weak 
   coffee.

6. **Closing** — encouraging wrap-up and one simple next step to try.

## Formatting requirements

- Use Markdown headers (H2 for sections, H3 for sub-sections)
- Use numbered lists for brewing steps, bullets for equipment
- Bold key terms on first use
- Include a short glossary (3–4 terms, e.g., "bloom," "extraction") as a 
  bulleted list near the top or bottom

## Tone & style

- Friendly, encouraging, jargon-free (explain any term you use)
- No brand endorsements — keep equipment recommendations generic
- Write for someone who has never brewed coffee manually before
```
