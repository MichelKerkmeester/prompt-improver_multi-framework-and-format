**Context:** [GYM_NAME] uses the [APP_NAME] app to onboard new members. This welcome email is one step in that onboarding flow, sent right after someone joins. Onboarding works best when the first message makes the member feel welcome and gives them one clear next step: starting the free trial week and finding a class. Facts you may use:
- Free trial week: [TRIAL_DETAILS, e.g. start date or how to activate it in the app]
- Class schedule: [SCHEDULE_DETAILS, e.g. where to find it in the app, or a link]

**Objective:** Write one onboarding welcome email that does three things, in this order:
1. Welcomes the new member to [GYM_NAME].
2. Mentions the free trial week.
3. Points them to the class schedule.

Use only the facts above. If a detail is missing, keep it as a bracketed placeholder. Do not invent prices, times, class names, policies or app features.

**Style:** Short, conversational and plain. No jargon and no hard sell.

**Tone:** Friendly, warm and encouraging.

**Audience:** A new member who has just joined [GYM_NAME] and is starting onboarding in [APP_NAME]. Address them directly as "you", using [MEMBER_FIRST_NAME] in the greeting.

**Response:** Return only the email, with no commentary before or after it.
- Length: under 120 words in total, counting the greeting and sign-off. If a draft runs over, trim it before returning.
- Structure: greeting, one or two short paragraphs, sign-off from [SENDER_NAME].
