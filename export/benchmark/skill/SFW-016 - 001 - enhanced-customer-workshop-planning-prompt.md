**Context:** A payroll software company is hosting a one-day, in-person customer workshop in Utrecht on 12 November for 25 HR managers from existing customer accounts. The day runs 09:30 to 16:00 and centers on the company's new leave module, delivered by two internal trainers. Attendees already use the core payroll product but are new to this module.

**Role:** Corporate training and event program designer with expertise in B2B software customer workshops, hands-on adult-learning session design, and trainer readiness planning.

**Action:**
1. Draft a full run-of-show for 09:30 to 16:00 that includes: an opening welcome/intro, two hands-on sessions on the new leave module (each with its own learning objective and activity, not a repeat of the same content), a lunch break, and a closing Q&A. Give a time allocation for every block and a one-line facilitation note (what the trainers do, what attendees do) for each hands-on session.
2. Draft a short prep checklist for the two trainers covering materials, room/tech setup, and a rehearsal or coordination step so both hands-on sessions run smoothly back-to-back.
3. Build in explicit moments that support the workshop's two success metrics: a quick feedback capture point after each hands-on session (to support an average rating of 8/10 or higher) and a clear, low-friction pilot sign-up call-to-action before the close (to support at least 10 sign-ups for the leave module pilot).

**Format:** Two deliverables in one response:
- A time-blocked run-of-show table with columns Time, Segment, Duration, Owner, Notes.
- A prep checklist as a short bulleted list grouped under Materials, Room/Tech, and Rehearsal/Coordination.

**Target:** The plan succeeds if it is realistic to execute within the fixed 09:30-16:00 window with two trainers, and if its design (pacing, feedback moments, sign-up moment) gives the workshop a credible path to an average session rating of at least 8/10 and at least 10 sign-ups for the leave module pilot.
