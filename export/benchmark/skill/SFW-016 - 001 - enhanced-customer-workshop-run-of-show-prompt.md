**Context:** [Company] is a payroll software company hosting a one-day, in-person customer workshop in Utrecht on 12 November for 25 HR managers from existing customer accounts. The purpose is to build confidence in, and drive adoption of, a new leave management module through hands-on practice rather than a lecture-style session. The day runs 09:30–16:00 and must include two hands-on sessions on the leave module, a lunch break, and a closing Q&A. Two internal trainers will co-facilitate and need to walk in fully prepared. This run-of-show will be used directly to plan and execute the day, so every block needs a clear time, purpose, and owner.

**Role:** Act as a B2B SaaS customer-enablement workshop designer experienced in running hands-on software training sessions for HR and payroll audiences.

**Action:** Produce two linked deliverables:
1. A minute-by-minute run-of-show from 09:30 to 16:00 covering: welcome/intro, two distinct hands-on sessions on the leave module, lunch, and a closing Q&A. Sequence the two hands-on sessions so they build on each other (for example, core setup and everyday use in the first, more complex or edge-case scenarios in the second) rather than repeating the same content. For each block, state its objective, the activity participants will do, and its duration.
2. A short prep checklist for the two trainers to complete before the workshop, covering: demo environment and materials readiness, room and tech setup, split of responsibilities between the two trainers during each block, and a rehearsal or dry-run reminder.

Build in two touchpoints the design depends on: a moment to capture a session rating from participants after each hands-on session (or a single end-of-day survey covering both, if that fits the flow better), and a clear, explicit call-to-action to sign up for the leave module pilot during the closing Q&A.

**Format:** Markdown with two clearly labeled sections:
- "Run-of-Show": a table with columns Time, Duration, Segment, Objective/Activity, Owner (Trainer 1 / Trainer 2 / Both).
- "Trainer Prep Checklist": a bulleted list grouped under "Before the Workshop," "Day-of Setup," and "During Sessions."

**Target:** Design every block so the day is positioned to hit two measurable outcomes: an average participant session rating of at least 8 out of 10, and at least 10 sign-ups for the leave module pilot by the end of the day. In the run-of-show, mark where the rating-collection moment(s) and the pilot sign-up ask sit, and briefly note why that placement supports the target (e.g., asking for sign-ups immediately after hands-on practice, while confidence in the module is highest). Where a detail was not specified (workshop year, exact split of hands-on topics, feedback collection method), state the assumption made in-line so it can be corrected before use.
