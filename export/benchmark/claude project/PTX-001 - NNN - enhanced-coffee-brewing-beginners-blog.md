**Context:** Many beginners give up on home brewing or settle for bitter, weak coffee. Most guides bury the few variables that matter under gear talk and jargon. This blog post should get a first-time brewer to a good cup quickly and build their confidence.

**Objective:** Write a blog post that teaches complete beginners how to brew good coffee at home. By the end, the reader should be able to make one cup with [brew method, default: French press] and know what to change if it tastes off. For each fundamental, explain why it changes the taste before giving the instruction. Cover these fundamentals:
- Coffee-to-water ratio
- Grind size
- Water temperature
- Bean freshness
- Brew time

- Use short paragraphs and descriptive subheadings.
- Give the brewing steps as a numbered list.
- Give measurements in both metric and US units, as starting points rather than rules.

**Tone:** Friendly, encouraging and unpretentious. Avoid coffee snobbery and assume no prior knowledge.
