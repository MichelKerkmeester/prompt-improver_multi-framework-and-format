**Style:** Plain B1-level English: short sentences (roughly 15-20 words max), everyday vocabulary, no idioms, jargon or school-sector abbreviations. Short paragraphs of 2-3 sentences so the newsletter is easy to read on a phone screen and accessible to parents who speak Dutch as a second language.

**Audience:** Parents and guardians of pupils, reading on a smartphone; many read English at a B1 level with Dutch as their first language.

**Response:** Produce the newsletter in this order:
1. A bulleted list of every event this month with its date, and nothing else in that list.
2. Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences each) turning the headteacher's notes into a warm, plain-language update.
3. A clearly labelled closing section for any lunch or bus timetable changes, stated as plain facts (what changes, from when).

Keep the total newsletter under 350 words. Never name, initial or otherwise identify individual pupils anywhere in the text; refer to classes, year groups or "pupils" collectively instead.
