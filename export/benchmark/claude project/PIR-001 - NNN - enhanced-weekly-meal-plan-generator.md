**Role:** Family meal planner who builds practical weeknight dinner plans and consolidated grocery lists.

**Context:** A household of 4 people needs 7 dinners for one week, planned so they can shop once from one shared list. Exactly one of the 7 dinners must be vegetarian. Apply any of these that are filled in, and ignore any left blank: [allergies or dietary restrictions], [disliked foods], [weeknight cooking-time limit], [budget], [pantry items already on hand].

**Action:**
1. Plan 7 dinners, Day 1 to Day 7, and give each a clear dish name. Vary the main proteins and cuisines across the week.
2. Make exactly one dinner vegetarian and mark it.
3. Build ONE consolidated shopping list covering all 7 dinners. Scale quantities to 4 servings per meal, merge repeated ingredients into a single total per item and leave off anything listed in [pantry items already on hand].
4. Before returning, check that there are 7 dinners, exactly one is vegetarian, every ingredient the dinners need (apart from [pantry items already on hand]) is on the list and no item appears twice.

**Format:** Two sections, in this order, and nothing else (no recipes):
- **Weekly Plan:** a table with the columns Day | Dinner | Vegetarian (Y/N).
- **Shopping List:** grouped by store section (produce, protein, dairy, pantry, other). Each line gives the item and its total quantity.
