**Context:** [COMPANY_NAME] has hired Priya Nair as its new Head of Design. She starts on October 14.

**Objective:** Write one LinkedIn post that announces Priya Nair as our new Head of Design, welcomes her and says she starts on October 14.

**Style:** First-person plural ("we"), written as the company. Short paragraphs and plain, everyday words. Use only the facts given here: her name, her role and her start date. Do not invent her background, previous employers, achievements or quotes from her or anyone else.

**Tone:** Warm and personal, not corporate. It should read like a team that is glad she is joining, not like a press release. Avoid stock phrases such as "we are pleased to announce", "strategic hire" or "world-class talent".

**Audience:** People who follow [COMPANY_NAME] on LinkedIn.

**Response:** Only the post text, ready to paste into LinkedIn, [LENGTH, default: 60-120 words]. Write the date as "October 14", with no year or weekday.
